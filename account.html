Keepers

Keep your own list of birds

A keeper account remembers which birds you follow and the support plan you put together, so you do not have to build it again on your next visit. It takes one form and no money.

This is a student project, not a real service

The account is kept in this browser only, in localStorage, and the password is stored as a short hash of what you type. Nothing is sent anywhere and nobody checks it. Never type a password that you use on a real website. “keeper1234” is a good one to use here.
