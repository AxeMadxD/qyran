Rules of the house

No flash
Photos yes, flash no. In the owl aviary please put the phone away completely.
Quiet voice
A frightened eagle breaks its own feathers against the net. Noise costs the bird weeks.
No feeding
Every bird is weighed and its food is measured to the gram.
No hospital block
Patients never see visitors. A bird that gets used to people cannot be released.

How to get here

From Oskemen it is a day

  1. Oskemen to Katon-Karagay

    370 km of good asphalt, about five hours. Buses leave at 08:00 and 14:00.

  2. Katon-Karagay to Berel

    58 km, one hour. The last 12 km are gravel and fine for any car in summer.

  3. Berel to the centre

    Qarasai street, the last house before the river. Look for the yellow gate and the wind sock.
