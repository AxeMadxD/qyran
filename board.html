The route of every patient

From the box to the sky

Every bird goes the same way: treatment, the forty metre flight corridor, release. The corridor takes three birds at a time, so this board is where the vet decides who flies next.

The board

Meat to cut every day

for the birds that are still here

A new call

Admit a bird

A new patient always starts in treatment. Its name must be different from every bird on the board, because the name is how the volunteers find its box and its food in the fridge.

The tag list and the meat counter on the board change as soon as the bird is added.
