Which bird is wider?

People bring us “a small hawk” and it turns out to be an eagle with a two metre wingspan. Look at the bird on the left and guess if the next one has wider or narrower wings.

How to play

Guess
Wider or narrower than the bird on the left? The wingspan of the next bird is hidden until you choose.
Same size
If both birds have the same wingspan, any guess counts as right.
Mistakes
A wrong guess costs one life and the streak starts again from zero. No lives left means the end of the game.
Keyboard
Arrow up for wider, arrow down for narrower, arrow right for the next bird.

Why a game

Size is the first thing people get wrong

A shepherd who knows that a golden eagle is wider than a man is tall will not try to carry it in a shopping bag. Twenty species in the game live in the Altai or fly over it, and every number is the largest wingspan of an adult bird, rounded to five centimetres.
