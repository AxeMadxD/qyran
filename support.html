Support

One eagle eats 24 000 tenge a month

We have no state money. The centre runs on tickets, adoptions and people who drive an injured bird a hundred kilometres for free. Here is everything you can actually do.

Three ways to help

Most urgent

Report a bird

If it cannot fly away from you, it is injured. Call, keep it dark and warm, do not feed it. Every hour matters more than any donation.

+7 705 214 00 19

Monthly

Adopt a resident

Nine birds live here for good. An adoption covers the meat, the vitamins and the vet for one of them, and you get its weight chart every month.

See the three levels

Hands

Volunteer

Cleaning aviaries, cutting meat, building perches, driving. Two days a month, from May to October, no experience needed.

Other things we need

Freezer space
We buy quail twice a year and need somewhere to keep it.
A second transport crate
Large, plastic, with a steel door. About 45 000 ₸.
Drivers
Anybody who can bring a box from Katon-Karagay to Berel on a weekday.
Old towels
Honestly. We use dozens of them every month.

Write to us

One form for everything

Found a bird, want to adopt, want to volunteer, want to bring a school — it all goes to the same table in the office, and the duty vet reads it every evening.

If the bird is bleeding, tangled in a net or lying under a power line, do not write. Call the rescue line, it is answered day and night.
