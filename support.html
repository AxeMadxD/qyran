Support

One eagle eats 24 000 tenge a month

We have no state money. The centre runs on tickets, adoptions and people who drive an injured bird a hundred kilometres for free. Here is everything you can actually do.

Three ways to help

Most urgent

Report a bird

If it cannot fly away from you, it is injured. Call, keep it dark and warm, do not feed it. Every hour matters more than any donation.

The four questions we are always asked

To the bird you choose, and the monthly report is about that bird: its weight, what it ate, what the vet wrote. The only shared part is the kitchen — we buy quail for the whole centre twice a year and take your bird’s share out of it.

You get the release report with the date, the ring number and the place, and your name is on it. After that you can move the support to another patient or stop it — a released bird costs us nothing, which is the whole point.

The three residents, yes: Keeper and Guardian include a visit on a closed day, with the falconer. The nine patients, no. A wild bird that gets used to a human face cannot be released, and that is more important than any visit.

Write to us

One form for everything

Found a bird, want to adopt, want to volunteer, want to bring a school — it all goes to the same table in the office, and the duty vet reads it every evening.

If the bird is bleeding, tangled in a net or lying under a power line, do not write. Call the rescue line, it is answered day and night.
